# Crunch-
CRUNCH+ is a proposed ₹59 protein-forward Indian snack designed for everyday snacking.  The idea is simple: create a snack that sits between traditional packaged snacks and expensive fitness-focused protein products.  

The Business Problem

Health-focused snacking is growing in India, but there is still a gap between:

Traditional snacks that are inexpensive but often lack a strong health proposition
Premium protein/fitness products that can be expensive for everyday consumption

The project asks:

Can a protein-forward snack be priced at ₹59 and still offer enough value for consumers to choose it repeatedly?


Purpose:

Discovery + impulse purchase + repeat purchase

Gyms & Sports

Use sampling and small-format distribution to generate product trial.

Offices & Campuses

Target the existing snack occasion rather than creating a completely new consumption habit.

Retail

Expand into modern trade and neighbourhood stores after demand has been validated.

Promotion Strategy
Campaign

"Snack Break, Upgraded"
